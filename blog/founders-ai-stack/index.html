The Founder's AI Stack:
10 Tools, One Lean Business

6 min read Tool Stack · UK Pricing May 2025 No paid placements

This is the current stack. Not the stack I recommend in the abstract — the one that's actually running a solo content and digital products business in the UK right now. What each tool does, what it genuinely costs in GBP, and what I'd cut first if the budget needed to shrink.

How to read this

Tools marked Essential are the ones I'd keep even if I had to cut everything else. Tools marked Optional earn their place but have free alternatives. UK pricing shown — note that many US-based tools bill in USD, so the GBP cost fluctuates slightly.

The Full Stack

01
Claude (Anthropic)
Primary AI for writing, reasoning and coding. Every first draft, every strategic brief, every bit of code I need — it starts here. Claude follows structured prompts more reliably than any other model I've used, and its outputs need less editing. The R-C-F framework was built around how Claude handles Role, Context, Format instructions.
EssentialDaily use
~£15/mo
Pro plan
02
Notion + Notion AI
The operating system for everything. Content calendar, project tracker, prompt library, client database, article drafts — all in one place. Notion AI is additive rather than transformative (Claude does the heavy AI lifting), but the "improve writing" and "summarise" features save time on editing. The free plan is genuinely usable; Plus is worth it for unlimited AI prompts.
EssentialWorkspace
~£12/mo
Plus plan
03
Make.com
Automation backbone. Connects everything that doesn't natively talk to each other. Current active automations: Instagram analytics → Notion database, website sign-ups → email list, saved articles → content calendar. One properly built Make workflow replaces 20–30 minutes of daily manual work. Steeper learning curve than Zapier but significantly more powerful per pound.
EssentialAutomation
~£8/mo
Core plan
04
Perplexity AI
Research engine. Replaces the 15-tab research spiral. For anything requiring current information — recent tool launches, news, stats, regulatory updates — Perplexity returns cited, synthesised answers in under a minute. The Pro plan's deeper search mode is worth it. I use it daily before writing any tool review or comparison.
EssentialResearch
~£17/mo
Pro plan
05
Descript
Video and audio editing by editing text. Record a video, Descript transcribes it, and you edit the video by deleting words from the transcript. Filler word removal is automatic. For anyone producing video content without a dedicated editor, this is the single biggest time saver. The AI voice clone feature means I can fix audio mistakes without re-recording.
VideoOptional
~£20/mo
Creator plan
06
Otter.ai
Meeting transcription and note-taking. Joins Zoom/Meet calls, transcribes in real time, and generates a summary. The summary isn't always perfect but it's always useful — it catches things I'd have missed. I feed the transcript into Claude after calls to extract action items in a structured format. Free tier covers most needs.
MeetingsOptional
Free
Free tier
07
ElevenLabs
AI voiceover for video content. When I need narration for short-form video without recording, ElevenLabs generates realistic speech from text. The quality gap between this and human recording has closed dramatically in 2025. I use it for tutorial-style content where on-camera presence isn't needed. The free plan has limited characters — the Starter plan is worth it for regular video output.
AudioOptional
~£4/mo
Starter plan
08
Midjourney
Visual content generation. Used sparingly — mostly for article header visuals, concept illustrations and carousel background textures. Not a replacement for photography or brand design, but for supporting visuals in digital content it's fast and consistent. The v6 quality is strong enough that I rarely need to iterate more than twice.
VisualsOptional
~£8/mo
Basic plan
09
Beehiiv
Newsletter platform. Manages the weekly email: subscriber list, template builder, analytics, and monetisation. The analytics are better than Mailchimp's for content-first newsletters — open rate by post, scroll depth, click maps. The free plan supports up to 2,500 subscribers. Scale plan unlocks boosts and paid subscriptions if you get there.
EssentialEmail
Free
Scale: ~£33/mo
10
ChatGPT (GPT-4o)
Secondary AI for research and creative variants. Claude is my daily driver, but ChatGPT earns its place for two specific use cases: anything requiring current web information (its browsing is more integrated), and generating multiple creative variants when I need tonal range — like five different caption hooks with distinct emotional angles. I don't use it for long-form writing or complex reasoning.
Secondary AIOptional
~£17/mo
Plus plan

£101/month for a full solo-founder AI stack. That's less than most businesses spend on a single SaaS subscription that's actually used by one person. The return on just the time savings — conservatively 5+ hours a week — makes it straightforward to justify.

What I'd cut first

If the budget needed to shrink to £50: keep Claude, Notion, Make.com, and Perplexity. Drop everything else. Those four tools handle 80% of the value. The optional five are quality-of-life improvements, not necessities.
