Brand Guidelines

What I will
and won't do.

Coetz.ai's value is built on honest, unsponsored-feeling content. These guidelines protect the audience relationship that makes sponsorships effective in the first place.

I will always
  • Disclose all paid partnerships clearly
  • Only promote tools I have actually used
  • Give an honest assessment — including limitations
  • Maintain creative control over all content
  • Deliver content on agreed timelines
  • Provide performance data post-campaign
  • Integrate sponsorships naturally into useful content
I won't
  • Publish content I haven't reviewed for accuracy
  • Promote tools I don't believe in
  • Guarantee follower counts, clicks, or sales
  • Accept scripts I must read verbatim
  • Run more than one paid post per week
  • Promote competitors to current sponsors
  • Alter my editorial voice to suit a brief
